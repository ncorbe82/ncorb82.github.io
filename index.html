Pebbles!

Queen of Willoughby Avenue and Muppet enthusiast

Pebbles was born on November 9th, 2013 and adopted on May 20th, 2023. She is a Shih Tzu and a very, very good girl. Until someone rings the doorbell.

At 12 lbs, Pebbles isn't intimidating, but that doesn't stop her from attempting to be a guard dog.

Some of Pebbles's favorite things include:

  • Any form of affection
    • Cuddles and kisses
  • The Muppets
    • Especially the Swedish Chef
  • Stealing socks
    • ...and hiding them
  • Being as close to people as possible
    • She's a tripping hazard
  • Fighting kibble
    • There's no explaination for this one

No matter how silly she can be, Pebbles is an excellent dog.
